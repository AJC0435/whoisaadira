01 NONLINEAR DYNAMICS The Lorenz Attractor +

PROJECT / 001

The growth of chaos from calm.

The Lorenz system is a set of three coupled, nonlinear differential equations. Under suitable parameter values, its trajectories form a characteristic butterfly-shaped strange attractor.

This project explores the system computationally using Python, tracing its trajectory through three-dimensional phase space and examining how its behaviour evolves over time.

02 NONLINEAR DYNAMICS The Rössler Attractor +

PROJECT / 002

A different geometry of chaos.

The Rössler system is another three-dimensional nonlinear dynamical system. Its equations can produce a distinctive spiral-like trajectory, providing a useful comparison with the Lorenz system.

This section documents the computational implementation, visualization, observations, and comparisons developed during the coursework.
